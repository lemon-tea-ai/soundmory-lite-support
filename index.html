How Soundmory Lite works

Local by design

No account, ads, or cloud processing. Recording, playback, and exports happen on your devices. Share only when you choose.

Listen your way

Browse by day and hour. Jump to a mark. Seek, skip, and replay. Export a whole day, individual hours, or original files with metadata.

A one-time purchase

Soundmory Lite is an audio-only app with no subscription or processing credits. Transcription and AI summaries are not included.

Requires Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later to record, paired with an iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Recording duration depends on battery, storage, and system interruptions. Record with any required consent.
