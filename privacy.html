Privacy policy · Effective November 13, 2026

This policy covers Soundmory Lite for iPhone and Apple Watch, provided by Ke Zhu (lemon-tea-ai).

What the app stores

Soundmory Lite stores audio you choose to record and related information, such as recording times, durations, marks, local diagnostics, and transfer receipts, on your devices. The microphone is used on Apple Watch for recordings you start.

What we collect

Soundmory Lite does not send your recordings or recording metadata to the developer or a developer-operated server. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, cloud transcription, or cloud processing service. The developer does not sell your recordings or use them to train AI models.

Transfer between your devices

The app uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to transfer audio and related metadata from your Apple Watch to your paired iPhone. Transfers are managed by the operating system and depend on device connectivity and availability.

Exports and backups

You can export audio and metadata through the system share sheet. When you select another app, person, storage provider, or cloud destination, that recipient's handling of the exported data is governed by its own practices and your settings.

Your Apple device or computer backup settings may include app data. Soundmory Lite does not provide its own cloud backup service or override your system backup settings. Review those settings if you want to control whether local app data is included in a backup.

Retention and control

Recordings remain in the app's local storage and may also exist in copies you export or back up. The Watch may retain audio while delivery is pending or for local recovery. You can manage microphone access in system settings. Removing an app can remove its local data, so export anything you want to keep first. Exported copies and device backups must be managed separately in their respective destinations.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads, and system services under its own policies. Apple may provide developers with App Store reports or system diagnostic reports according to your Apple settings. These do not give the developer access to recordings stored inside Soundmory Lite.

This website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure its service under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public and is used to respond to your request. Please do not include private recordings or personal information. Soundmory Lite does not require a GitHub account; only posting a support issue does.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above. For questions, use the support page and avoid including personal data in public issues.
