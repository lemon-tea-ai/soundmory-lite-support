Privacy policy · Effective November 15, 2026

This policy covers Soundmory Lite for iPhone and Apple Watch, provided by Ke Zhu (lemon-tea-ai).

What the app stores

Soundmory Lite stores audio you choose to record and related information, such as recording times, durations, marks, local diagnostics, and transfer receipts, on your devices. The microphone is used on Apple Watch for recordings you start.

What we collect

Soundmory Lite does not send your recordings or recording metadata to the developer or a developer-operated server. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, cloud transcription, or cloud processing service. The developer does not sell your recordings or use them to train AI models.

Transfer between your devices

The app uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to transfer audio and related metadata from your Apple Watch to your paired iPhone. Transfers are managed by the operating system and depend on device connectivity and availability.

Exports and backups

You can export audio through the system share sheet. If you choose to share a support report, it contains technical diagnostics rather than your recorded audio. When you select another app, person, storage provider, or cloud destination, that recipient's handling of the exported data is governed by its own practices and your settings.

Your Apple device or computer backup settings may include app data. Soundmory Lite does not provide its own cloud backup service or override your system backup settings. Review those settings if you want to control whether local app data is included in a backup.

Retention and deletion

Saved iPhone recordings remain until you delete them or enable automatic cleanup; separate copies may exist in your exports or backups. In Settings, you can choose days or hours to delete, delete all saved iPhone audio, or enable automatic cleanup for 1–365 days. Automatic cleanup is off by default. When enabled, it keeps today and the previous N minus 1 days for a setting of N days, runs when the app opens or syncs, and preserves clips that cross the cutoff. Clips crossing midnight are deleted in full, including the portion on the adjacent day.

The iPhone retains a small local deletion record, including the clip identifier, an identity fingerprint, deletion time, and deletion reason, to prevent deleted clips from returning through delayed transfers. This record does not contain the audio and is not sent to the developer.

Previously unreceived Watch audio may still arrive later. The Watch may retain temporary audio copies while delivery is pending or for local recovery. Deleting saved audio on iPhone does not remove copies you have exported or backed up; manage those separately in their destinations.

You can manage microphone access in system settings. Removing an app can remove its local data, so export anything you want to keep first.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, downloads, and system services under its own policies. Apple may provide developers with App Store reports or system diagnostic reports according to your Apple settings. These do not give the developer access to recordings stored inside Soundmory Lite.

This website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical request information to operate and secure its service under GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email soundmory@gmail.com, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please share only what is needed to help resolve your issue.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public and is used to respond to your request. Please do not include private recordings or personal information. Soundmory Lite does not require a GitHub account; only posting a support issue does.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The effective date appears above. For questions, email soundmory@gmail.com or visit the support page. Avoid including personal data in public issues.
