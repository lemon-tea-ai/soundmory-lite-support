A little help.
Back to your day.

Soundmory Lite records on Apple Watch. Your iPhone is where you listen, organize, and export.

Get started

  1. Install Soundmory Lite on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch.
  2. Open the Watch app. Tap the microphone control and allow microphone access.
  3. Tap during recording to mark a moment. Hold the control for at least 1.2 seconds to stop.
  4. Keep the devices connected. Open the iPhone app to see received audio in Today.
  5. Play a day, an hour, or a mark. Earlier days appear in Library.

Common questions

Where is my audio?

Recordings start on your Watch and transfer to the paired iPhone. Transfer can take time. Check Today and Settings → Refresh connection. Keep both apps installed and the devices nearby with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi available. Do not uninstall an app to troubleshoot missing audio: this can remove its local data.

Can I record throughout the day?

The app supports longer, user-started recording sessions. Actual duration depends on Watch battery, free storage, and system interruptions. Check the recording state periodically and charge your devices as needed.

How do I export?

On a day with saved audio, tap the Settings gear, then Export audio. Choose Whole day so far (or Whole day for an earlier date), Export all hours, or an individual hour. Only audio already received on iPhone is exported. Combined files omit unrecorded time gaps.

Does Lite transcribe or summarize?

No. Lite focuses on recording, bookmarks, playback, and audio exports. No model download or subscription is required.

Does it work offline?

Recording and playback do not use a cloud processing service. Watch-to-iPhone delivery needs a working connection between your paired devices. The destination you choose when exporting may have its own internet requirement.
